# Output template

Fill this structure. Write prose — state the finding and its reason in sentences. Do not pad with bullet lists where a sentence does the job, and do not include a section you have no data for; say what you could not read instead.

Numbers always arrive with a justification. "Stop at 29480" is incomplete; "stop at 29480, above the swing high that formed the manipulation leg" is the deliverable.

---

## 1. Bias and DOL

Direction for the session, the target level, and the FVG reasoning from the higher timeframes.

State which of Daily / 4H / 1H you actually read and whether they agree. Give the near DOL as a specific price with its timeframe, and separate it from any far structural DOL. If the 15m check changed the read, say what it showed.

If the timeframes disagree or the picture is unclear, that is the output — say there is no clean read today rather than forcing a direction.

## 2. Key levels

Two or three, never more. Each one gets: type (FVG / CISD / RJB), timeframe, price or zone, and why it is valid right now — unmitigated, freshly swept, or aligned across timeframes.

## 3. iFVG confirmation status

Which manipulation leg is being tracked, the highest gap timeframe inside it, and whether the body close has printed: confirmed, not yet, or still waiting for price to reach the level.

If unconfirmed, say plainly what has to happen — "waiting on a body close through the 4m gap at X" — and do not proceed to a plan as though it had.

## 4. Trade plan, if and when confirmation arrives

Entry, stop, and target, each with its reasoning. Express the target as R:R against the stop, aimed at the nearest sensible local high/low rather than the full distance to the DOL.

For risk sizing, ask the user for their risk percentage or per-trade amount if they have not given it. Never propose a currency position size yourself.

## 5. Daily limit status

How many trades have been taken today, how many remain under the one-to-two rule, and whether the current time falls inside 09:30–11:00 ET. If the window has closed, say so and frame the whole briefing as preparation for the next session.

Note any high-impact economic events in or near the window — or state that the calendar could not be checked.

## 6. Disclaimer

Close every complete briefing with the substance of this, in your own words, without softening it:

> This is not financial advice. This skill is an analysis structure built on public ICT/SMC concepts — it makes no performance claims and does not guarantee profit. Trading futures carries substantial risk of loss. Every entry, stop, and size decision is the user's own.
